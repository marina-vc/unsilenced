HOW ARE YOU FEELING?

CASE 1 OF 4 VIOLENCE PERPETRATED BY AN INTIMATE PARTNER
31.8%

17.7% of women in Europe have experienced physical or sexual violence by a partner. When psychological violence is included, that number rises to 31.8%. Controlling where you go. Monitoring who you talk to. Isolating you from the people you love.

For most of them (14.6%), it happened more than once.

And in the last 12 months alone, 5.3% of women across the EU lived through it. That's not the past. That's now.

31.8% of women in Europe, in an everyday situation.
