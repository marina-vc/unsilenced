CASE 1 OF 4 VIOLENCE PERPETRATED BY
AN INTIMATE PARTNER
17.7%

17.7% of women in Europe have experienced physical or sexual violence by a partner.

31.8%

When psychological violence is included, that number rises to 31.8%.

Controlling where you go. Monitoring who you talk to. Isolating you from the people you love.

14.6%

For most of them (14.6%),
it happened more than once.

And in the last 12 months alone, 5.3% of women across the EU lived through it.

That's not the past.
That's now.

CASE 2 OF 4 VIOLENCE PERPETRATED BY
PEOPLE OTHER THAN

20.2%

More than 1 in 5 women (20.2%) have experienced physical and/or sexual violence by someone other than a partner.

Usually someone they knew.
Someone from their circle.

CASE 3 OF 4 SEXUAL HARASSMENT

30.8%

1 in 3 women (30.8%) in Europe has experienced sexual harassment at work in their lifetime.

41.6%

It's not always obvious. Sometimes it's a comment. A look. A joke everyone laughs at.

And most of them smiled.
Stayed quiet.
Afraid to lose the job.
